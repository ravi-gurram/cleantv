# Asset sources

- `app/assets/alpine.jpg` and `coast.jpg`: original AI-generated scenery created for CleanTV, 1920×1080. Bundled locally; no background network requests.
- YouTube: Iconify `logos:youtube`, from https://github.com/gilbarbara/logos (CC0).
- Netflix and Prime Video: Simple Icons 11.15.0, https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons/tree/11.15.0/icons (CC0). Transparent PNG copies are trimmed exports of the supplied vectors.
- Apple TV and Hulu: Simple Icons 5.24.0, https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons/tree/5.24.0/icons (CC0). Hulu PNG is a trimmed export.
- Disney+: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Disney%2B_logo.svg ; white lettering adapted from the supplied vector for a dark field.
- Inputs and down arrow: Tabler Icons via Iconify, https://github.com/tabler/tabler-icons (MIT), license in `app/assets/TABLER-LICENSE`.

App logos remain trademarks of their respective owners and identify the apps launched by CleanTV.
